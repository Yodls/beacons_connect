{# The icon set, defined once and referenced with . The small icons are Material Symbols (Rounded, weight 400), by Google, under the Apache License 2.0: the plain outline shapes people already know from the apps on their phones. They are filled with currentColor, so they take the colour of the text around them. The five taskbar icons carry a filled shape as well, shown only on the tab you are on (the stylesheet sets --ic-fill there). The "big-" icons (Home's three shortcuts), the lighthouse and the game icons are still pixel art with their own palette (UMass Boston navy, Beacon Blue, sky, orange and yellow).
