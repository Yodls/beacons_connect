{# Post a meetup, inside the campus map's window (_community_map.html), so the pin and the form are one thing. The map script shows this box when its "Post a meetup" key is pressed and puts a draggable pin on the map; the script at the foot of find.html keeps the pin and the fields in step (choosing a place moves the pin to its landmark, dragging the pin rewrites the "Near ..." line). Cancel, Escape or posting puts it away again. There is no "where" to pick: the pin is the where. The server keeps the pin's words (the nearest landmark, "Near Campus Center") as the meetup's place. Without JavaScript the box is simply here, under the map, with the pin line, the note and Cancel hidden: the form posts as it always did and the meetup goes on the Campus Center, where the pin would have started. Two positions in here are asserted rather than chosen: {# Shown only for a write-in, and rendered plainly so the form still works with no JavaScript. #} {# The day as a date, and the time as three choices. AM/PM starts blank on purpose -- a preselected one is exactly the guess that makes a meetup's time ambiguous -- and the server refuses an hour without it. The date is required: the meetup's chat ends after it. The time beside it stays optional. #} {# One group, not three boxes: a single border with hairlines between, so the hour, the minutes and the half of the day read as one time and cannot be split across two lines by wrapping. #}
