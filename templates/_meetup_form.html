{# Post a meetup, inside the campus map's window (_community_map.html), so the pin and the form are one thing. This is the one way a student puts anything on the map: a meetup for right now or for a date, for everyone, for friends they pick or for one of their clubs. The map script shows this box when its "Post a meetup" key is pressed and puts a draggable pin on the map; the script at the foot of find.html keeps the pin and the fields in step (dragging the pin rewrites the "Near ..." line) and shows only the parts of the form that the choices made so far call for. Cancel, Escape or posting puts it away again. It is laid out as the Meet up form in a chat is (_meetwin.html): a section each for what, when, who can see it and a note, under a small caption, ruled off from the next. The captions and the people counter are that form's own, shared in _meet_parts.css. The choices are switches (one frame, a part for each choice, the chosen part filled): radio buttons, so the form posts the same names and values with or without a script. There is no "where" to pick: the pin is the where. The server keeps the pin's words (the nearest landmark, "Near Campus Center") as the meetup's place. Without JavaScript the box is simply here, under the map, with the pin line, the note and Cancel hidden and every part of the form showing: it posts as it always did, the server reads only the parts that go with what was chosen, and the meetup goes on the Campus Center, where the pin would have started. Who it can be for (meet_friends, meet_clubs), how long "Now" can run (meet_durations) and the types a write-in can be (meet_kinds) come from the page (find_game), so nothing here waits on the map's script.
