{# The campus map (Leaflet + OpenStreetMap), drawn as a Beacon OS window. How it works (static/community-map.js does all of this by id): - Flags come from data-flags-url. Tap one and its card opens in a callout standing on the flag (a Leaflet popup); on a phone it opens as its own window under the map instead, in place of the "Post a meetup" key, which comes back when it is shut. - Flags on the same spot (a meetup with no pin goes on its place's landmark) show as one flag with a count; a tap opens a list of them in the same window a flag's card uses, and a row opens that flag's card, with a key back to the list. - Every pin a student puts here is a meetup's, made by posting the meetup: from this page, Community Chat, a club or a friend chat. It is seen by whoever the meetup is for (everyone, a club's members, the friends its author picked, or the two people in a one-on-one), and its author can drag it to move it, lock it to the people who are in, or cancel the meetup from its card. - The key to the flags' colours is a strip on the map's bottom edge, across the window: what the colours mean belongs with the map, not beside whatever happens to be under it. It is written here from map_kinds, so it is there with the page. On a phone it steps aside with the "Post a meetup" key, while the form or a flag's card has the room under the map. - On /find (map_meetup set by the page) there is a "Post a meetup" key. It is written in its row under the map, and the script stands it on the map's bottom right corner wherever the map has its full height (from 701px), leaving nothing under the flags strip, so the window ends there; on a phone it stays in its row, across the pane. It opens the meetup form (_meetup_form.html) under the map with a draggable pin already on the map, so the map and the form share one screen. The key is revealed by the script once the map is up; without JavaScript the form is simply there. The Post tab in the bottom bar arrives with ?post=1 (data-post), and the page's script opens the form as soon as the map is ready. data- attributes give the script what only the server knows: the API, the landmark logos (UMass Boston, the Healey Library sign, UMass Boston Recreation), where a meetup's chat, its "I'm in" and its cancelling live (with 0 standing in for the id), and a flag to open straight away (?flag=, used by the "See on map" links on meetup cards). Keep every id and data- attribute here: the script depends on them. Styles are in _community_map.css, included by find.html's page style. #} {% import "_ui.html" as ui %}
